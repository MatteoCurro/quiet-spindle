From **Venezia Mestre station** you cannot reach **St Mark’s Square** with one direct land vehicle. The two main gateways into the historic centre are **Venezia Santa Lucia** if you take the train and **Piazzale Roma** if you use bus or tram. From either point you continue on foot or by waterbus.

The most practical choice depends on departures, luggage and how much you want to walk. Compare the complete route rather than only the first leg into Venice.

## Main options

- **Train to Venezia Santa Lucia + walk.** A straightforward option if you are already at the station and want to enter Venice by rail.
- **Train to Venezia Santa Lucia + waterbus.** Useful if you want to reduce the walking section in the historic centre.
- **Bus or tram to Piazzale Roma + walk.** Piazzale Roma is Venice’s terminal for road transport.
- **Bus or tram to Piazzale Roma + waterbus.** A practical combination if you are already using the ACTV urban network.

## Which option should you choose?

If you are already at **Venezia Mestre station**, the train to **Venezia Santa Lucia** is often the first route worth comparing. If you are already using ACTV urban transport, **Piazzale Roma** may be the more natural gateway.

Then choose how to handle the final section. Walking lets you experience Venice directly; the waterbus can reduce walking but adds boarding and a separate fare. There is no single best option for everyone.

## Practical tips

- Check the route shortly before leaving because waiting times and service conditions can change.
- With heavy luggage, consider changes, bridges and steps rather than map distance alone.
- Do not look for a bus or tram “direct to St Mark’s Square”: road transport stops at **Piazzale Roma**.
- If you want to limit walking, compare a route that uses the waterbus after Santa Lucia or Piazzale Roma.
- Consider the entire journey, not just the fastest first leg.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there a direct train from Mestre to St Mark’s Square?

No. Trains from Venezia Mestre arrive at **Venezia Santa Lucia**, not St Mark’s Square. From Santa Lucia you continue on foot or by waterbus.

### Can I take a bus or tram directly to St Mark’s Square?

No. Buses and trams from the mainland terminate at **Piazzale Roma**. From there you continue through the historic centre.

### Is Santa Lucia or Piazzale Roma better?

It depends on your complete journey. Santa Lucia is the train arrival point; Piazzale Roma is the road-transport terminal. Both allow you to continue on foot or by water.

### Which option is easier with luggage?

Prefer fewer changes and evaluate the walking section carefully. Bridges and steps can matter more than the apparent distance.

### How long does the whole trip take?

The total varies with the first transport leg, waiting, any waterbus connection and walking. Use the live route planner rather than relying on one fixed time.
