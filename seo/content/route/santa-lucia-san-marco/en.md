From **Venezia Santa Lucia station** to **St Mark’s Square**, you can walk across the historic centre or use the waterbus to reduce the pedestrian section. You are already inside Venice, so there is no need to take a bus or tram to “enter” the city.

The right choice depends mainly on luggage, available time and whether you want to experience the journey on foot.

## Main options

- **Walk.** Cross the historic centre from the station to San Marco without another transport ticket.
- **Waterbus + walk.** Reduces the walking section and can be useful with luggage.
- **Mixed route.** Depending on current departures, walking to a different landing stage before boarding can make sense.

## Which option should you choose?

If you are travelling light and want to see Venice, walking is a natural choice. If your priority is reducing bridges and distance on foot, the waterbus can be more comfortable.

For visitors, navigation has a specific fare. If you expect more waterbus journeys later in the day, compare a single ticket with the time-based passes.

## Practical tips

- When you leave Santa Lucia, you are already on the Grand Canal.
- With luggage, count bridges rather than only walking minutes.
- If using the waterbus, check which San Marco stop is best for your destination.
- If you expect more ACTV journeys, compare a single ticket with a pass.
- Do not confuse Venezia Santa Lucia with Venezia Mestre.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much is the waterbus from Santa Lucia to San Marco?

The price table shows the standard visitor navigation fare and the main ACTV passes. The fare applies to the ticket product rather than one specific Santa Lucia–San Marco route.

### Can I walk without taking any transport?

Yes. Walking is a classic way to cross the historic centre and does not require another ticket.

### Does a mainland bus ticket work on the waterbus?

No. Visitor navigation requires an ACTV ticket that includes water transport.

### Can I use an ACTV pass I already have?

Yes, if your pass includes navigation and is still valid under its conditions.

### Which option is easier with luggage?

The waterbus can reduce walking, but it may not remove every bridge on the final section. Compare the route to your exact destination.
