From **St Mark’s Square** to **Rialto Bridge**, walking is usually the simplest option. The two landmarks are close within the historic centre and connected by one of Venice’s busiest pedestrian corridors.

The waterbus can make sense if you are already using ACTV navigation or if the journey is part of a longer route, but for the direct San Marco–Rialto connection walking is often more straightforward.

## Main options

- **Walk.** The most natural connection between the two areas and no transport ticket is needed.
- **By water.** Useful if you already have a valid pass or are combining this leg with a wider waterbus itinerary.
- **Alternative walking route.** At very busy times, a slightly different path through the calli can feel easier.

## Which option should you choose?

For most visitors already in San Marco, walking is the practical choice. The main benefit of the waterbus is not distance but the ability to include this section in a wider day based on water transport.

If you already hold a valid ACTV pass, using the waterbus may not add any extra cost. If you would need to buy a single navigation ticket only for this short connection, walking is much cheaper.

## Practical tips

- Use **Rialto Bridge** as the exact destination, not simply “Rialto”.
- The calli between San Marco and Rialto can be very crowded.
- With an ACTV pass, consider water transport as part of a wider itinerary.
- If you also want Rialto Market, check the exact point on the map.
- With luggage, even a short pedestrian route can become less comfortable.

## Frequently asked questions

### How far are St Mark’s Square and Rialto Bridge?

They are close within the historic centre and usually linked on foot.

### Do I need the waterbus?

No. It is not necessary for this direct journey, although it can fit a wider ACTV itinerary.

### How much is the waterbus between San Marco and Rialto?

The normal visitor navigation fare applies. If you already have a valid pass, another ticket may not be necessary.

### Does the mainland urban ticket work here?

No. The mainland ticket does not include visitor waterbus travel.
