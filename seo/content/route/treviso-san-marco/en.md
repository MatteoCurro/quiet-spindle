Travelling from **Treviso Canova Airport (TSF)** to **St Mark’s Square** is best understood as a two-stage journey: first the ground transfer into Venice, then the final trip through the historic centre. ATVO airport buses reach **Piazzale Roma**; from there you can continue on foot or by waterbus depending on luggage, mobility and the exact location of your accommodation.

## Main options

- **ATVO bus to Piazzale Roma plus walking.** Straightforward if you travel light and want to avoid buying another transport ticket.
- **ATVO bus plus waterbus.** Useful with luggage or when you want to reduce walking, although it adds a transfer at Piazzale Roma.
- **Bus to Mestre plus train and local connection.** Possible in some cases, but usually involves more steps than the direct airport bus into Venice.
- **Taxi or private transfer to Piazzale Roma.** Much more expensive, but potentially useful for groups or difficult arrival times.

## Which option should you choose

If you are travelling light and do not mind walking, continuing on foot from Piazzale Roma can be an enjoyable first view of Venice. With large suitcases, children or reduced mobility, a waterbus may make the journey easier, even though a short walk can still remain at the end.

The key point is that “Venice” and “St Mark’s Square” are not the same destination. Airport buses stop where road traffic ends, while St Mark’s is deeper inside the pedestrian city. That final leg can materially change the total travel time and effort.

## Practical tips

- Set St Mark’s Square or your hotel as the real destination in the planner.
- Check the nearest waterbus stop to your accommodation before choosing the second leg.
- With luggage, consider bridges, steps and walking distance.
- Airport bus departures can respond to flight operations; check the service on your travel day.
- For the return to TSF, include the time needed to reach Piazzale Roma before the airport bus.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there a direct bus from Treviso Airport to St Mark’s Square?

Not all the way to the square. The airport bus connects Treviso Canova with Venice Piazzale Roma. From there you must continue through the historic centre on foot or by waterbus, so planning the complete route matters.

### Is it better to walk or take a waterbus from Piazzale Roma?

It depends on luggage, mobility and your preferred pace. Walking avoids another ticket, but Venice’s bridges and narrow streets can be tiring with suitcases. A waterbus reduces walking but adds a transfer and another fare.

### Can I travel through Mestre instead?

Yes, but it is not automatically faster. Going via Mestre adds at least one connection and makes sense mainly when bus and train times align well or when you need to stop in Mestre for another reason.

### How early should I leave St Mark’s for Treviso Airport?

There is no single safe number for every traveller. First allow time to reach Piazzale Roma or Mestre, then add the airport transfer and a separate buffer for check-in and security.
