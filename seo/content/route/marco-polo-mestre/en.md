To travel from **Venice Marco Polo Airport (VCE)** to **Venezia Mestre Station**, you can stay entirely on the mainland and use a direct ground connection. This is usually the most sensible approach if you are staying in Mestre, catching a train or using Mestre as your base, because there is no reason to travel first to Venice’s historic centre.

## Main options

- **ACTV bus.** ACTV airport services connect Marco Polo with Mestre and the local urban network, which can be useful if you need another bus or tram afterwards.
- **ATVO Airport Bus Express.** ATVO publishes a direct airport connection to Mestre FS designed for travellers and luggage.
- **Taxi or private transfer.** This can make sense for groups, heavy luggage or late arrivals, although it costs much more than a public bus.
- **Private car.** Useful mainly when Mestre is an intermediate stop before continuing elsewhere in Veneto.

## Which option is best

If your hotel or train is close to Mestre Station, a direct airport bus to the station area is normally the simplest choice. If your destination is elsewhere in Mestre, compare where each service actually stops and how easy the final urban connection will be.

Going first to Venice Piazzale Roma and then travelling back to Mestre is usually inefficient. Mestre Station is also a major rail hub, so arriving there directly is convenient for onward trains towards Padua, Verona, Treviso and many other destinations.

## Practical tips

- Check the exact Mestre stop printed on your ticket or timetable.
- If you are connecting to a train, leave a buffer between bus arrival and train departure.
- With heavy luggage, favour a service stopping close to the railway station.
- Do not confuse Venezia Mestre with Venezia Santa Lucia; they are separate stations.
- For the return to VCE, confirm the current departure stop and timetable on the day.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there a direct bus from Marco Polo Airport to Mestre?

Yes. Both ACTV and ATVO provide airport connections towards Mestre. The most useful arrival point depends on the service and your final address, so check whether the bus serves Mestre Station or another stop on the mainland network.

### How long does the airport-to-Mestre journey take?

Journey time depends on the selected service and road traffic. Use the current timetable rather than a fixed estimate, and add extra time if you have a train connection or another time-sensitive booking.

### Do I need to go through Venice to reach Mestre?

No. Marco Polo Airport and Mestre are both on the mainland. Travelling first to Piazzale Roma and then back towards Mestre normally adds unnecessary distance, cost and transfers.

### Can I continue by train from Mestre?

Yes. Venezia Mestre is one of the main railway hubs in Veneto. If you are connecting by train, allow enough time to leave the bus, enter the station and reach the correct platform.
