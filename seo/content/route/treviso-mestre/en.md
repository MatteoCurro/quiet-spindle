From **Treviso Canova Airport (TSF)** to **Venezia Mestre Station**, a direct airport bus is the simplest option for many travellers. Mestre is useful both as a mainland accommodation base and as a major interchange for trains, buses and trams, so there is usually no need to continue into Venice if Mestre is your actual destination.

## Main options

- **ATVO Airport Bus Express.** The airport service links Treviso Canova with Mestre and Venice and serves the railway-station area.
- **Bus plus train via Treviso.** This may work at certain times, but it introduces a transfer and should be judged on the complete journey.
- **Taxi or private transfer.** More expensive, but useful for groups, late arrivals or addresses far from Mestre Station.
- **Private car.** Mainly relevant when Mestre is only one stop in a longer trip.

## Which option should you choose

If you are staying near Mestre Station or connecting to a train, the direct airport bus reduces transfers and is easier with luggage. If your accommodation is in another part of Mestre, check the onward urban connection from the station because a local bus or tram may be preferable to a long walk.

Travelling via Treviso Centrale can be competitive only when the timetables line up well. Do not compare only the in-vehicle travel time: include the wait, the transfer and the time required to move between the airport and Treviso railway station.

## Practical tips

- Confirm that your selected service actually stops at Mestre FS.
- Leave a reasonable buffer if you are connecting to a train.
- Airport departures can be adjusted around flight operations.
- Treviso Airport and Treviso Centrale are different places.
- For your return, check the correct ATVO boarding point in Mestre and the current timetable.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there a direct bus from Treviso Airport to Mestre?

Yes. ATVO publishes an Airport Bus Express between Treviso Canova, Mestre and Venice. Mestre Station is one of the important stops, making it convenient for nearby hotels and onward rail connections.

### Is the direct bus better than going through Treviso Centrale?

The direct bus usually avoids an extra transfer. Going through Treviso Centrale may make sense only when the local bus and train times align particularly well. With luggage, the direct airport service is generally easier to manage.

### Can I catch a train immediately after arriving in Mestre?

Yes, but avoid a very tight connection. Road traffic can affect the bus arrival time, and you still need time to enter the station, identify your platform and reach it with your luggage.

### Does the airport bus operate normally when flights are delayed?

Airport departures may be modified around flight operations. ATVO advises travellers to check the current service, so confirm the timetable on the day if your connection is important.
