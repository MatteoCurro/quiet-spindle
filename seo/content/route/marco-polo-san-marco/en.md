There is no single best route from **Venice Marco Polo Airport** to **St Mark’s Square**. You can enter Venice by land, arriving at **Piazzale Roma** and continuing on foot or by waterbus, or choose an airport water connection toward the historic centre.

The right option depends on luggage, budget, your preferred arrival point and how many changes you want to make.

## Main options

- **Airport bus to Piazzale Roma + walk.** A clear and relatively simple combination.
- **Airport bus to Piazzale Roma + waterbus.** Useful if you want to reduce the walking section after the land transfer.
- **Water connection from the airport.** Convenient when the served stop is close to your final destination.
- **Mixed routes.** Depending on live departures, a combination of modes may work better.

## Which option should you choose?

If price matters most, compare the airport bus first with direct water services. If you have heavy luggage, number of changes and the final walking section may matter more than a small fare difference.

A water service can be more direct for some areas, but it is not automatically door-to-door. St Mark’s Square is still reached from a nearby landing stage, often with some walking.

## Practical tips

- Use **St Mark’s Square** as the final destination rather than only “Venice”.
- Check the exact arrival stop of any airport water service.
- If you choose the bus, include the onward section from Piazzale Roma.
- With luggage, compare changes and bridges as well as price.
- For the return trip, allow enough margin beyond the transfer itself.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does it cost from Marco Polo Airport to St Mark’s Square?

It depends on the mode. The current visitor fares for ACTV airport bus products and Alilaguna are shown in the price table on this page.

### Is there a direct water service from the airport to San Marco?

Airport water services operate toward Venice. Check the actual stop served and how far it is from the square.

### Is the bus cheaper than the water connection?

Usually the airport bus costs less than a direct water transfer, but reaching San Marco may then require a second ticket or a longer walk.

### Which option is easiest with luggage?

The route with fewer changes may be more comfortable even if it costs more. Compare the complete journey to your final address.

### Can I buy one airport + waterbus ticket?

Combined airport and ACTV navigation products exist. Check the current conditions and validity before purchase.
