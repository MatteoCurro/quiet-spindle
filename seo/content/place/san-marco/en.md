**St Mark’s Square** sits in the heart of Venice’s historic centre and cannot be reached directly by car, bus or tram. If you are arriving from the mainland, the two main gateways are **Venezia Santa Lucia** station and **Piazzale Roma**. From either point you continue on foot or by waterbus.

If you are already in Venice, the best option depends on where you start, how much you want to walk and whether you have luggage. It is more useful to compare the complete route to the square than just the first transport leg.

## How to reach St Mark’s Square

- **From Venezia Santa Lucia.** Walk through the historic centre or combine the journey with a waterbus to reduce the distance on foot.
- **From Piazzale Roma.** This is the main road-transport terminal; continue on foot or by water.
- **From Mestre.** First reach Venice by train, bus or tram, then complete the final section from the historic centre.
- **From Marco Polo Airport.** Compare a land transfer into Venice with water-based airport connections, taking luggage and your exact destination into account.
- **From elsewhere in Venice.** Walking and waterbus are the two main options to compare.

## What to know before you go

St Mark’s Square is central, but bridges, crowded calli and luggage can make a walking route feel longer than it appears on a map. If you want to experience Venice on foot, the walk can be part of the visit. If you prefer to reduce walking, compare a route that uses the waterbus toward the San Marco area.

## Practical tips

- Search for **St Mark’s Square** as the final destination, not simply “Venice”.
- If you arrive by train, use **Venezia Santa Lucia** as the historic-centre gateway.
- If you arrive by bus, tram or car, **Piazzale Roma** is the main reference point.
- With luggage or a stroller, consider bridges and steps as well as distance.
- Check the route shortly before leaving, especially if you plan to use water transport.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I take a bus directly to St Mark’s Square?

No. Buses and trams stop at **Piazzale Roma**. From there you continue on foot or by waterbus through the historic centre.

### Which train station is best for St Mark’s Square?

**Venezia Santa Lucia** is the train station in the historic centre. From there you can walk or take a waterbus toward San Marco.

### Is it better to walk or take the waterbus from Santa Lucia?

It depends on luggage and how much you want to walk. Walking lets you cross Venice directly; the waterbus reduces the pedestrian section but adds boarding and a fare.

### How do I get to St Mark’s Square from Mestre?

Reach Venice first, usually via Santa Lucia by train or Piazzale Roma by bus or tram, then continue on foot or by waterbus.

### Can I walk all the way to St Mark’s Square?

Yes, if you are already in the historic centre. From the mainland you must first cross into Venice by public transport.
