**Piazzale Roma** is the main road-transport arrival point for Venice’s historic centre. Buses, trams and many mainland connections terminate here. From this point onward, the journey continues mainly **on foot** or by water.

Think of Piazzale Roma as a transfer hub rather than a final destination. If you are heading to St Mark’s Square, Rialto, Santa Lucia or a hotel in the historic centre, compare the complete route from here.

## How to continue from Piazzale Roma

- **To St Mark’s Square.** Walk across Venice or use the waterbus to reduce the pedestrian section.
- **To Venezia Santa Lucia.** The station is nearby and normally reached on foot.
- **To Rialto.** Continue through the historic centre on foot or combine the journey with water transport.
- **To a hotel.** Check the exact address because a short map distance may still include several bridges.
- **Back to the mainland.** Piazzale Roma is the key hub for buses, trams and road connections.

## What to know when you arrive

Piazzale Roma is the last major road hub before pedestrian Venice. Looking for a bus “closer to San Marco” is therefore the wrong approach: once you arrive here, the journey changes to walking, bridges and water transport.

If you travel with luggage, compare the route to your actual accommodation rather than just the distance to the nearest landmark.

## Practical tips

- Use Piazzale Roma as an intermediate hub when arriving from the mainland.
- Check your hotel location before starting to walk.
- With heavy luggage, consider a route with fewer bridges.
- Santa Lucia is close enough to compare a direct walking transfer.
- For San Marco and Rialto, compare walking with the waterbus.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Piazzale Roma already in Venice’s historic centre?

Yes. It is the main road-transport terminal at the edge of the historic centre.

### Can I walk from Piazzale Roma to St Mark’s Square?

Yes. The entire journey can be walked, although it includes several bridges.

### Is Venezia Santa Lucia close to Piazzale Roma?

Yes. The station and Piazzale Roma are neighbouring gateways and usually connected on foot.

### Can I drive all the way into Venice from Piazzale Roma?

No. Road vehicles do not continue through the historic centre. From Piazzale Roma you proceed on foot or by water.
