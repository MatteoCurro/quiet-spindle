**Venice Marco Polo Airport** is on the mainland, not in the historic centre. To reach Venice you must choose a connection toward **Piazzale Roma**, Mestre or a water-based route, depending on your final destination.

If you are travelling to St Mark’s Square or a hotel in the historic centre, compare the whole journey. The first airport transfer is only one part of the trip.

## How to continue from Marco Polo Airport

- **To Venice/Piazzale Roma.** Land connections take you to the main road terminal, where you continue on foot or by water.
- **To St Mark’s Square.** Compare a land transfer plus the historic-centre section with an airport water connection.
- **To Mestre.** Check the best direct mainland connection for the exact area where you are staying.
- **To Venezia Santa Lucia.** Plan the transfer into Venice and then the most convenient final link to the station.
- **With luggage.** Number of changes and walking distance can matter more than the theoretical journey time.

## What to know before you go

A common mistake is treating “Venice” as one single destination. **Piazzale Roma, Santa Lucia, St Mark’s Square and Mestre are different places**, and the best transfer changes according to the real address.

If you are staying in Mestre, for example, there is usually no reason to travel into the historic centre first.

## Practical tips

- Enter your final hotel or destination, not just “Venice”.
- If staying in Mestre, compare direct mainland connections.
- For St Mark’s Square, compare bus + historic centre with water transport.
- With luggage, consider changes, bridges and the last walking section.
- For the return journey, allow enough time beyond the transfer itself for airport procedures.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much is the bus from Marco Polo Airport to Venice?

The current visitor fare is shown in the price table on this page together with other airport options. Use it as a planning reference and check again before buying.

### Can I reach St Mark’s Square directly by water from the airport?

Airport water services connect Marco Polo with Venice. Check the actual stop served and how far it is from your final destination.

### Should I go through Venice if I am staying in Mestre?

Not necessarily. If your accommodation is in Mestre, compare direct mainland connections first.

### What is the difference between the airport bus and Alilaguna?

The bus connects the airport with road hubs such as Piazzale Roma or Mestre. Alilaguna uses water routes toward Venice and some islands.

### How early should I leave for the airport?

Do not rely on one fixed rule. Calculate the live route from your location, include transfers and then add enough time for check-in and security.
