**Treviso Antonio Canova Airport (TSF)** is not in Venice itself, but it has direct airport bus connections towards **Venezia Mestre** and **Venice Piazzale Roma**. If your destination is inside the historic centre, reaching “Venice” is only the first part of the trip: you should also account for the final walk or waterbus ride to your hotel, St Mark’s Square or another neighbourhood.

## Main onward options from Treviso Airport

- **For Mestre.** ATVO airport services stop in Mestre, including the railway-station area, which can be convenient for hotels on the mainland or onward rail travel.
- **For Venice.** Airport buses reach Piazzale Roma, the road terminal at the edge of the historic centre. From there you continue on foot or by waterbus.
- **For St Mark’s Square.** Treat Piazzale Roma as an interchange rather than your final destination and compare the complete journey.
- **With luggage.** A direct airport bus can be easier than an itinerary involving several transfers, especially after a flight.
- **For the return journey.** Start planning from your real accommodation and add a sensible margin for traffic, connections and airport procedures.

## Treviso Airport and Marco Polo are different airports

Treviso Canova and Venice Marco Polo are separate airports. TSF is in the Treviso area and the overland transfer to Venice is longer than from Marco Polo. Always check the airport code on your booking before buying a bus or private transfer.

If you are staying in Mestre, getting off there may save an unnecessary trip into Venice. If you are staying in the historic centre, Piazzale Roma is usually the practical road gateway before your final local connection.

## Practical tips

- Search for your actual hotel or destination instead of simply entering “Venice”.
- Check whether Mestre or Piazzale Roma gives you the easier final connection.
- With heavy luggage, compare walking distance and bridge crossings after the bus.
- Airport timetables can respond to flight operations, so confirm the service for your travel day.
- Use the TSF airport code when comparing tickets and transfers.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there a direct bus from Treviso Airport to Venice?

Yes. ATVO publishes airport services linking Treviso Canova with Mestre and Venice. In Venice the road arrival point is Piazzale Roma, so travellers heading to St Mark’s Square, Rialto or another district still need an onward walk or waterbus connection.

### Should I get off in Mestre or continue to Venice?

It depends on where you are staying. For accommodation in Mestre, continuing to Venice usually adds unnecessary travel. For the historic centre, Piazzale Roma is the normal road interchange and a useful starting point for the final leg.

### Is luggage included on the ATVO airport bus?

ATVO’s Airport Bus Express information states that luggage is included. Check the conditions attached to your current ticket before travelling, particularly for unusually large items or special baggage.

### How do I return from Venice to Treviso Airport?

The reverse airport service normally leaves from Venice or Mestre. Plan from your actual location, allow time to reach the departure stop and add a separate margin for check-in and airport security rather than relying only on the bus journey time.
