**Murano** is reached by water. From Venice’s historic centre, the main option is the ACTV navigation network; from **Marco Polo Airport**, you can also compare a direct Alilaguna connection to the island.

The best route depends on where you start and which Murano stop is closest to your actual destination. The island has several landing stages, so “Murano” is not one single arrival point.

## How to reach Murano

- **From Venezia Santa Lucia.** Choose a water route toward the Murano stop closest to your destination.
- **From Piazzale Roma.** Join the water network and continue toward the island.
- **From St Mark’s Square.** Compare available services and the most convenient departure stop in the central area.
- **From Marco Polo Airport.** Consider a direct Alilaguna connection or alternatives via Venice.
- **From other islands.** Check whether a direct service is available or a change is required.

## What to know before you go

Murano is not a single stop. Before leaving, check the exact address of your hotel, glass workshop, museum or restaurant and choose the most useful landing stage. Getting off at the wrong stop can add unnecessary walking and bridges.

If you expect to use the waterbus several times during the day, compare a single ticket with time-based passes. For visitors, the difference can have a real impact on the day’s budget.

## Practical tips

- Enter the precise destination on the island.
- For several waterbus journeys, compare a pass with repeated single tickets.
- From the airport, compare the direct water service with routes via Venice.
- Check the return route before starting your visit.
- With luggage, choose the landing stage closest to your accommodation.
