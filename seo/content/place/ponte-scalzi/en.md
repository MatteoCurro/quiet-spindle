**Scalzi Bridge** crosses the Grand Canal directly beside **Venezia Santa Lucia** station. It is one of the first bridges many travellers see when arriving by train and an important pedestrian link between the station area, Santa Croce and the opposite bank.

If you have luggage, first check whether you actually need to cross it. Many nearby hotels and transport connections are easier to reach by staying on the same side of the Grand Canal.

## How to reach Scalzi Bridge

- **From Venezia Santa Lucia.** The bridge is immediately outside the station area and easy to reach on foot.
- **From Piazzale Roma.** Walk toward the station along the pedestrian connection between Venice’s two main gateways.
- **From Cannaregio.** Check which side of the Grand Canal you are on before deciding whether to cross.
- **From San Marco or Rialto.** Walk through the historic centre or use a waterbus toward the Ferrovia area.
- **With luggage.** Consider the bridge steps before crossing unnecessarily.

## What to know before you go

Scalzi Bridge is mainly useful as a practical crossing for people arriving at or leaving the station. The best route depends heavily on what comes next. Automatically crossing the Grand Canal can add unnecessary steps if your hotel or stop is already on the correct bank.

For nearby accommodation, Santa Lucia or Piazzale Roma, always check the exact location before crossing.

## Practical tips

- Confirm which side of the Grand Canal your destination is on.
- Avoid unnecessary bridge crossings with heavy luggage.
- Use **Venezia Santa Lucia** as the main orientation point when arriving by train.
- For longer journeys, compare walking with the waterbus from the Ferrovia area.
- Allow extra space and time around the station at busy periods.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Scalzi Bridge close to Santa Lucia station?

Yes. It is directly associated with the station area and only a short walk from the main exit.

### Do I need to cross Scalzi Bridge to reach Piazzale Roma?

Not necessarily. The best route depends on your exact destination and which side of the canal you are already on.

### Is it easy to cross with luggage?

The bridge has steps, so heavy luggage can make it awkward. Check first whether crossing is actually necessary.

### How do I reach Scalzi Bridge from St Mark’s Square?

Walk across the historic centre or take water transport toward **Ferrovia/Santa Lucia**, then continue on foot.
