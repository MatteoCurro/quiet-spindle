**Venezia Santa Lucia station** is the main railway arrival point inside Venice’s historic centre. When you leave the station, you are already in Venice and can continue **on foot** or by waterbus toward St Mark’s Square, Rialto and many other areas.

The best choice depends on your final destination and luggage. For accommodation near the station, walking may be immediate; for more distant areas it is useful to compare the full walking route with water transport.

## Where to go from Santa Lucia

- **To St Mark’s Square.** Walk through the historic centre or use the waterbus to reduce the pedestrian section.
- **To Rialto.** The walking route is popular, while water transport can help with heavy luggage.
- **To Piazzale Roma.** The two gateways are close and normally connected on foot.
- **To Cannaregio.** Many destinations are easily reached without changing transport.
- **To the islands.** Check the most convenient water connection from Ferrovia or nearby stops.

## What to know when you arrive

Santa Lucia is not a suburban station. The Grand Canal is directly outside the building, so you can start exploring Venice immediately. This can be surprising if you expect buses, taxis and normal road traffic in front of the station.

With heavy luggage, do not judge convenience only by map distance. Bridges and steps can make a nearby hotel more demanding than it looks.

## Practical tips

- Check the exact address before crossing the Grand Canal.
- Count bridges as well as walking minutes when travelling with luggage.
- Do not confuse **Venezia Santa Lucia** with **Venezia Mestre**.
- For San Marco and other distant areas, compare walking and waterbus.
- Piazzale Roma is the nearby hub for buses, trams and road transfers.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Venezia Santa Lucia already in the historic centre?

Yes. The station is directly inside Venice’s historic centre on the Grand Canal.

### What is the difference between Venezia Mestre and Venezia Santa Lucia?

Mestre is on the mainland; Santa Lucia is in the historic centre. If your train continues to Santa Lucia, you arrive directly in Venice.

### Is it practical to walk from Santa Lucia to St Mark’s Square?

Yes if you are comfortable walking and travelling light. Otherwise compare a waterbus route to reduce the pedestrian section.

### How do I reach Piazzale Roma from the station?

Piazzale Roma is nearby and normally reached on foot using the connection between the two main gateways.
