**Venezia Mestre station** is one of the main gateways to Venice from the mainland. From here you can reach **Venezia Santa Lucia** by train, travel toward Piazzale Roma by urban transport or connect with the airport and central Mestre.

If you are staying in Mestre, the station can be a very practical way to enter and leave Venice without relying only on buses or trams. The best choice depends on your final destination and ticket.

## How to continue from Venezia Mestre station

- **To Venezia Santa Lucia.** Regional trains provide a straightforward way to enter the historic centre.
- **To St Mark’s Square.** Travel first to Santa Lucia by train or Piazzale Roma by bus/tram, then continue on foot or by waterbus.
- **To central Mestre.** Buses and trams connect the station with different parts of the city.
- **To Marco Polo Airport.** Compare available bus connections and allow enough margin for your flight.
- **To other cities.** Mestre is also a major rail interchange for onward travel.

## What to know before you go

**Venezia Mestre and Venezia Santa Lucia are not the same station.** Mestre is on the mainland; Santa Lucia is inside the historic centre. If your train continues to Santa Lucia and that is where you need to go, do not get off in Mestre.

For travellers staying in Mestre, however, the station can make daily trips into Venice simple and predictable.

## Practical tips

- Always read the full station name on your ticket.
- For the historic centre, compare train to Santa Lucia with bus/tram to Piazzale Roma.
- If you expect several journeys in one day, check integrated passes.
- With luggage, account for platforms, underpasses and possible lifts.
- For the airport, compare the complete connection rather than distance alone.

### Is there a day pass between Mestre and Venice?

The **Mestre Daily Pass** combines mainland urban transport with regional trains in the Venice urban area up to Santa Lucia. It does not include waterbuses.

### Is the train or tram better for reaching Venice?

It depends on your final destination. The train arrives at Santa Lucia, while bus and tram services reach Piazzale Roma.

### Is Venezia Mestre station convenient if I stay in central Mestre?

Yes, especially if your accommodation is near the station or well connected by bus or tram. From Piazza Ferretto, compare the urban leg to the station with direct road transport toward Venice.
