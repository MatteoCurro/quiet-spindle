**Piazza Ferretto** is the pedestrian heart of central Mestre. It is a useful base for travellers who want to stay on the mainland while keeping good connections with Venice, the railway station and Marco Polo Airport.

To reach it, think in terms of **Mestre city centre**, not Venice’s historic centre. From the station you normally use local transport; from Venice you can return by train, bus or tram depending on where you start.

## How to reach Piazza Ferretto

- **From Venezia Mestre station.** Use urban transport toward central Mestre and choose the stop closest to the square.
- **From Venezia Santa Lucia.** Travel to Mestre by train, then continue into the city centre.
- **From Piazzale Roma.** Bus and tram services connect Venice with the mainland; compare which option leaves you closer to central Mestre.
- **From Marco Polo Airport.** Compare connections toward Mestre and include the final section to Piazza Ferretto.
- **From central Mestre.** Once nearby, the square and surrounding streets are easy to explore on foot.

## What to know before you go

Piazza Ferretto is not immediately outside Venezia Mestre station. The two areas are in the same city but require a short urban connection. If you stay near the square, check whether your journey to Venice is more convenient via the station or by direct bus/tram.

Understanding this difference helps avoid unnecessary changes and makes it easier to choose the right ticket.

## Practical tips

- Search specifically for **Piazza Ferretto**, not simply “Mestre centre”.
- If arriving by train, include the station-to-square section.
- For daily Venice trips, compare rail with urban transport from your accommodation.
- If making several journeys, check the Mestre Daily Pass or suitable ACTV products.
- From the airport, use the exact final address rather than only “Mestre”.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Piazza Ferretto close to Venezia Mestre station?

It is not directly beside the station. Urban transport is often the easiest option, especially with luggage.

### How do I travel from Piazza Ferretto to Venice?

Reach Venezia Mestre station for the train to Santa Lucia or use bus/tram connections toward Venice and Piazzale Roma.

### Is Piazza Ferretto a practical base for visiting Venice?

It can be, especially if you want to stay in central Mestre and still have straightforward public transport into Venice.

### Which ticket is best for Mestre and Venice?

It depends on how often you travel and whether you will use waterbuses. The Mestre Daily Pass covers mainland transport plus urban rail, while ACTV water transport requires a product that includes navigation.
